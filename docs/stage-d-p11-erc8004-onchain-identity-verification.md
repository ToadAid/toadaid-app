## Goal

Make the frozen D-P9 seam honest in both directions **without any frozen change**: the receiver performs a read-only `ownerOf(agentId)` observation of the ERC-8004 identity registry (through two independent keyless public Base mainnet RPC endpoints at one explicitly pinned block), records it in a typed bounded observation adapter, and a separate verification contract re-inspects both legs — the frozen mapping assessor's fresh re-run and the observation classifier's fresh re-run — then demands exact field-by-field reproduction of the mapping's claimed verification evidence by the performed observation. The frozen seam stays hardcoded false forever; verification lives entirely in this cut's own vocabulary.

## Proof model

- **Performed observation, refused bases stay literal.** The observation's satisfy-able basis is `performed_receiver_observation`; the refused bases — `claimed_receiver_observation_not_performed`, `reported_by_third_party`, `inferred_from_cached_projection`, `replayed_from_prior_observation` — are all valid-but-unsatisfied: a type-safe claim literal is exactly the derived-evidence invalid path, and a replay is the attestation replay trap.
- **Two-endpoint rule, record-level.** The retrieval provenance carries both endpoint hosts, the count, `endpointAgreement` (`agreed`/`disagreed`/`not_established`) and honest detail on disagreement; an `agreed` record over fewer than two endpoints is malformed, not satisfied (one projection cannot agree with itself); `trustReviewState: "receiver_performed_two_endpoint_self_reviewed"` is conjunctive.
- **Recomputed digest leg (D-P8 precedent).** No crypto in either contract: `pondErc8004CanonicalObservationDigestLines` exports the one canonical serialization (15 fixed-order lines over the observed-fact fields); the receiver's script-side recompute (runner, selftest `node:crypto`) sha256s those lines and the classifier compares the claimed digest against that recomputed input — claimed-vs-recomputed, never claimed-vs-nothing. Any single-field flip is caught by the honest receiver's recompute.
- **D-P2 freshness, verbatim.** The observation event is diagnosed with the same ordering and literals as every Stage D cut — unknowns diagnosed for invalid inputs, future time refused, the fresh boundary inclusive — and a stale observation never renews the verification.
- **Independent re-inspection, then reproduction.** The verification re-runs the frozen D-P9 mapping assessor and the D-P11 observation classifier over the supplied records (no trust in prior assessments), restates the held-principal binding from the freshly validated record, and then — the heart — requires `chainIdObserved === canonicalSource.chainId`, `registryAddress === canonicalSource.registryAddress`, `agentId === observedAgentId`, `ownerObserved === performedOwnerRead.observedOwner`, `blockTag === observationMetadata.observedBlockTag`, all five fields non-null on both sides. The frozen all-null record is **valid** and established — what fails is the reproduction, so its refusal is `observed_evidence_does_not_reproduce_mapping_evidence`, never `mapping_record_invalid`.
- **Layered partiality (D-P10 mold), one documented deviation.** The mapping leg is satisfied when the record is valid and every unsatisfied frozen mapping check is one of the two legs this cut re-performs (binding via check 2, onchain verification via checks 3-5), so each refusal stays attributable to its own check; the receiver-observed literal is one conjunct (check 5), never the proof. One defensive seventh reason literal (`receiver_verification_proof_incomplete`) completes the ladder's totality and is unreachable by construction.
- **Historical point-in-time posture.** The observation carries `observedClaimPosture: "historical_point_in_time_observation_not_current_truth_not_future_ownership"` and `scopePosture: "read_only_observation_evidence_only_no_admission_no_grant_no_registration_no_signing"`; the record deliberately carries **no** `principalRef` — binding to the receiver's principal happens only through the separately governed D-P9 mapping.

## Honesty mechanism — the frozen seam stays hardcoded; verification never widens it

The honest positive states are this cut's own vocabulary: `performed_receiver_onchain_observation_recorded` (observation) and `fixture_structural_receiver_verified_against_performed_observation` (verification) — with the surface field honestly splitting `structural_fixture` from `live_base_mainnet_read_only_rpc`. No D-P11 assessment carries any frozen tuple name (`onchainVerificationState`, `mappingEstablishmentState`, `mappingEstablishesGrant`, `privateReadsActivated`, `erc8004IdentityAcceptedAsPrincipalId`, `onchainIdentityAcceptedAsAuthentication`) — the frozen D-P0…D-P10 family's literals stay the only carriers, proven by `hasOwnProperty` over every matrix arm and every fresh fail-closed run. The frozen mapping assessor, run against the positive arm's non-null claim, still reports `onchain_verification_not_performed` / `not_verified` / check 8 unsatisfied — the seam did not move. The D-P9 readiness composition is deliberately NOT re-run here: it hardcodes `receiverVerification: "not_performed"` (L167), and re-running it would imply verification widens readiness — the mapping stays insufficient by itself to establish a Grant (delegated-authority L313; attestation L427 "ERC-8004 registry record != local admission"). The live surface is CI-never: the runner and the selftest's env-gated live block (`TOADAID_LIVE_ERC8004_VERIFY === "1"`) share one code path, real observed values (owner, block tag) pin only under `scripts/`, and the live read honestly refuses verification of the receiver's own structural fixture mapping — which names no real NFT — with `observed_evidence_does_not_reproduce_mapping_evidence` (live proof is for the observation subject; `source_verified != wiring_verified != live_verified`). Stage D's heading stays `— BLOCKED`: collaborative and multi-principal reads keep the explicitly named deferred lane.

## Authority ceiling

The verification establishes exactly nothing outside its own vocabulary: on every arm `verificationEstablishesGrant: false`, `erc8004VerifiedBindingAcceptedAsPrincipalId: false`, `erc8004VerifiedBindingAcceptedAsAuthentication: false`, `verificationEstablishesMembershipOrAdmission: false`, `credentialAdmitted: false`, `personalMemoryContentAdmitted: false`, `currentTruthAdmitted: false`, `runtimeActivationPosture: "not_included"`, `authority: "none"` — and the observation's own ceiling (`observationAcceptsOwnerAsPrincipalId: false`, `observationEstablishesAdmission: false`) never flips on any arm. The observed owner is never a PrincipalId, an authentication, an admission, or authority; the reproduction is the D-P9 mapping's own check-8 demand made real, not a new authorization. A live observation of a real registered agent names a real owner address — and that address is historical point-in-time evidence only, pinned solely in the runner's runtime output, never in any frozen file.
