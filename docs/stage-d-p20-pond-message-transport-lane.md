## Goal

The receiver **records one transport policy — the in-process policy, the only performable policy this cut — over a currently prepared D-P17 delivery candidate**, assessed by one frozen ceremony (`assessPondTransportPolicyDecision`) whose core mechanism is the same house re-run: the frozen D-P17 `assessPondDeliveryCandidateDecision` over the 14 candidate keys at the SAME evaluation pair; the policy records only when the re-run verdict is `delivery_candidate_prepared_session_scoped_no_dispatch`. The recorded state is `transport_policy_recorded_session_scoped_no_external_transport`; the record is governance-only and is consumed by no transport stage this cut (`transportPolicyConsumedThisCut: false` pinned in the contract ceiling). In parallel, a **standing fail-closed inbound wall** (`assessPondRemoteMessageIntakeRefusal`) refuses every remote external message-intake claim on every path — there is no open branch, no intake log, and nothing stored. External transport policies (A2A, MCP, HTTP, websocket, queue, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and undeclared future transports) are DECLARED VERBATIM only to refuse at their own dedicated cause; actual transport execution stays upstream-governed and is not built.

## Load-bearing mechanism

- **The policy re-runs the certified candidate it governs.** The decision re-runs the frozen D-P17 `assessPondDeliveryCandidateDecision` over a dedicated 14-key projection (`fullCandidateLegs`), never the 15-key input spread, so the policy record itself is never handed to the D-P17 re-run — the D-P18/D-P19 mechanism one lane earlier per the lifecycle. The candidate is the frozen D-P17 candidate verbatim (dv preserved); the policy record is never re-declared from it.
- **Three-depth honest echoes.** `mappedCandidateState`/`Reason`/`Diagnosis`, `mappedEstablishmentState`/`Reason`/`Diagnosis`, `mappedReadGateState`/`Reason`/`Diagnosis`, and `mappedDeliveredRecordAdmissionState`/`Reason` — all computed on every arm BEFORE cause selection, typed via quoted-index reads through the frozen `PondDeliveryCandidateDecisionAssessment`. A refusal three depths down (retraction → gate refusal surfacing through the candidate's reassessment echo) stays readable at its own depth.
- **The policy event's own freshness leads after the reassessment — and the policy event is NOT the newest event.** `transport_policy_event_not_session_current` fires on the event's own D-P2-style diagnosis (`transport_policy_event_time_only` freshness basis) after the reassessment and performability checks. Recorded deviation (ladder-order honesty, the D-P18/D-P19 lesson): the policy event sits BETWEEN the delivery intent and the dispatch on the frozen fixtures, so the lifecycle order is provable — `est 60 000 < composed 61 000 < intent 81 000 < POLICY 84 500 < dispatched 85 000 < receipt 87 000 ≤ eval 90 000` — and freshness isolates by ladder order, not by newest-event arithmetic. Proven in both directions: a future policy event refuses on its own diagnosis while the leg echoes stay green, and a stale evaluation makes the legs refuse FIRST while the policy event's own diagnosis still carries the honest stale verdict verbatim.
- **The policy re-assesses after retraction (the deliberate contrast with the D-P19 receipts).** `transportPolicyRetentionPosture` (`transport_policy_is_module_state_process_lifetime_no_indefinite_retention_reassessment_honest_after_retraction`): unlike a D-P19 receipt — frozen at issuance, inspection-only, historical evidence — a transport policy is session-scoped GOVERNANCE whose applicability the reassessment governs. After retraction the policy row presents the reassessment's verbatim refusal (`confined_reassessment_refusal`); frozen survival would dress a dead policy as live governance. Both retention literals are pinned verbatim in the selftest in one block.
- **The declarative basis.** `receiver_recorded_transport_policy_not_inferred` is the only admitting basis; five refusal bases (inferred from the prepared candidate, inferred from a receipt, asserted by model completion, inferred from channel visibility, replayed from a prior policy decision) refuse at the declarative check with `receiver_transport_policy_proof_incomplete` — unsatisfied `["policy_basis_receiver_recorded_not_inferred"]`, satisfied 8, every leg echo green (L49-55).
- **One policy per delivery decision is receiver-recorded module state.** `ui/pond-transport.js` refuses a repeat policy over the same delivery decision by recording the basis `replayed_from_prior_policy_decision` — the ceremony then refuses at the declarative basis check. A refused attempt stores nothing. An out-of-range delivery index refuses before any input exists (`delivery_decision_index_out_of_range`, no assessment fabricated).
- **The scope check quoted-iterates the validated candidate.** `transport_policy_event_not_of_the_current_session_scope`: scope is `recorded_at ≥ established_at AND ≥ composed_at AND ≥ recorded intent time`, read via quoted indexes through the validated candidate's own metadata (no re-declared field names anywhere in the mechanism).
- **The wall is a standing fail-closed wall with no open branch.** The state set is ONE literal, `remote_message_not_intaked`, pinned compile-time (single-literal invariant). Four causes: claim-shape invalid (`remote_message_claim_invalid`) → a claimed trusted channel class (`remote_message_claimed_trusted_channel_class` — even the DECLARED intake class, `conversation_context`, refuses when a remote claim self-declares it: the receiver's declared intake vocabulary says what ITS channels deliver, never what a remote sender may self-declare) → unknown class fail-closed (`remote_message_channel_class_unknown_fail_closed`, trusted-channel L35) → the terminal refusal (`remote_message_intake_refused_no_remote_agent_admission`) for the honestly self-declared `untrusted_external_input` claim: two honest checks read (well-formed, in vocabulary), the intake-declaration and sender-admission checks never open, because no remote-admission path exists this cut. No session legs, no clock, no records — the wall exists before any session.
- **The claim carries no content field, and the wall echoes nothing.** `PondRemoteMessageClaim` is 3 exact keys (`claimedTransport`, `claimedChannelClass`, `senderEvidenceRef`) — no content, envelope, MessageId, or serialization field, honoring the L240/L241 deferrals at shape level. The assessment carries ZERO `claimed*` fields (transport is not agent, admission L78); `claimContentEchoed`/`claimContentStored`/`senderIdentityEchoed`/`senderIdentityAcceptedAsIdentity` all false, pinned by the all-false ceiling family.
- **The intake-channel-class declaration is a frozen constant, not an input.** `POND_STAGE_DP20_DECLARED_INTAKE_CHANNEL_CLASSES = ["conversation_context"]` — a declaration input with no declaration record was incoherent; the declaration is receiver-recorded module frozen state instead (recorded deviation).
