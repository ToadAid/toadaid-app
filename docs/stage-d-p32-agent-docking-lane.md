## Goal

Make the docking door honest and visible: the Agents view gains one additive panel, **the docking bay**, showing for each of two fixture agents — Community Scout (`agent:community:sample-generic`, `remote_external_agent`, admission `unknown`, the same stranger the D-P25 front face projects) and Lore Mason (`agent:project:sample-loremason`, `community_agent`, admission `not_established`) — the canonical admission ceremony they would have to pass: the identity evidence they present (A2A AgentCard, MCP or service identity, deployment identity — all `declared, not verified`), the six sequence steps (all `declared, not performed`), the requested scope relationship (`declared, not granted`), and the declared capabilities (`declared, not granted`); plus one standing-walls block stating who may admit, that no decision is made here, that standing intake is refused by default for an unadmitted sender, the profile interlock, the untouched consequence path, and revocation/e-stop dominance. Nothing docks here yet — no listener, no intake channel, no admission backend, no docking operation control: the panel has no selector and takes no input. It answers the user's question ("how agent dock to toadaid app?") in the only way the current law permits: fully, in presentation.

## Load-bearing mechanism

- **Exact-shape, fail-closed dossier seam.** A docking brief is valid only with the exact 12-key shape (contractVersion `pond-agent-docking-lane-d-p32` literal, `kind: "pond-agent-docking-brief"`, bounded `agentId`/`displayName`, vocabulary membership for `profile` (2 values: `community_agent | remote_external_agent` — admission-law profiles only) / `admissionState` (4 values reusing the D-P25 state vocabulary), bounded distinct `presentedRef`s, ≤ 8 evidence entries of the exact 3-key shape (`evidenceClass` in the 8-value canonical vocabulary, `verificationBasis: "declared_not_verified"`), ≤ 32 distinct bounded capability refs, and the three pinned posture literals (`dockingPosture`, `runtimeActivationPosture`, `authority`)). Extra or planted keys (a planted `credential` refuses), unknown evidence classes, duplicate evidence refs, duplicate capability refs, wrong versions or postures, or unbounded strings refuse the whole brief at `docking_brief_input_invalid` — and every refusal echoes NOTHING (`presentedBrief: null`, empty sequence and evidence, no dossier mirror-back; the selftest asserts the planted value leaves no trace in the serialized refusal).
- **The ceremony is contract-frozen.** The six sequence steps derive from `POND_STAGE_DP32_SEQUENCE_STEPS` (the canonical admission-sequence ids verbatim at 53de8698), each rendered `stepLabel` + `stepPosture: "declared_not_performed"`; the standing walls are the same frozen `walls` object on green and refused arms alike — the law does not change when the input does.
- **The all-false ceiling as type and runtime both.** `PondStageDP32DockingAssessment` carries the 12-key ceiling (`admissionDecisionMade`, `admissionRecordIssued`, `evidenceVerified`, `agentIdIssued`, `scopeRelationshipEstablished`, `standingIntakeOpened`, `membershipCreated`, `grantIssued`, `capabilityAuthorized`, `credentialStoredOrProjected`, `messageComposedOrDelivered`, `agentInvoked`), compile-time pinned all-false by `PondStageDP32Invariant_AllEffectCeilingsFalse` and `PondStageDP32Invariant_ActivationAndAuthorityRemainAbsent` (`runtimeActivationPosture: "not_included"`, `authority: "none"`), with `dockingChangesPresentationOnly: true` re-asserted by the selftest on every arm including the refusal arm.
- **Deterministic render ceremony (the established esbuild shape).** `scripts/render-stage-d-agent-docking-lane.mjs` bundles the record module with a stdin entry re-exporting the real assessor — esbuild 0.28.2, ESM, `platform: "browser"`, `target: ["es2022"]`, unminified with `keepNames`, no timestamp, per-line trailing whitespace stripped, `--check` byte parity — producing the committed `ui/generated/pond-stage-d-agent-docking-lane.js` + provenance JSON (`renderedStages: ["D-P32"]`, inputs naming contract + fixture). The selftest drives the SAME assessor from the generated bundle and asserts the generated record carries no transport/administrative primitive (`admit`, `verify`, `openIntake`, `connect`, `send`, `deliver`, `invoke`, `grant`, `join` all absent).
- **Fill-once, inspection-first renderer.** `ui/pond-agent-docking-lane.js` renders strictly over the committed record: version tie (`data-docking-record-version === contractVersion`), the record's `runtimeActivationPosture`/`authority` literals pinned, textContent-only output, no selector and no event listener (docking is presentation, not an operation), fail-closed posture (`data-docking-render-posture="fail_closed"`, briefs withheld) on any missing node, wrong version, or invalid record, a per-brief refused panel (`docking-brief-refused`, dashed, echoing only the refusal reason) when a committed brief stops validating while the rest of the lane stays presented, and no `fetch(`, `WebSocket`, `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, or `innerHTML` anywhere in the module.
