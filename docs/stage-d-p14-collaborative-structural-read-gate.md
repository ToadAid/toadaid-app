## Goal

Retire the Stage D collaborative-reads blocker with one full gate cut, zero ui changes. A receiver-owned three-contract read gate: **(1)** a counterpart join declaration ceremony — the receiver explicitly declares a standing session join with a second principal ref that is pre-existing-by-declaration and carries nothing; **(2)** a collaborative activation — one explicit, session-scoped, restart-expiring, non-grant receiver transition over the two declared principals; **(3)** a collaborative read admission — a fail-closed multi-principal read over a 13-label structural target table, admitted only inside the activation. The gate's core is the **no-second-identity widening proof**.

## Proof model

- **One held ref per leg.** The gate's chain re-runner takes exactly one held principal ref and one record set per call — the no-second-identity widening proof's structural precondition. It re-runs, per principal: the D-P5 binding ceremony, the D-P6 authentication observation, the D-P8 verifier and challenge-proof, the D-P9 issuance, the D-P9 ERC-8004 mapping (pinned `receiverVerification: "not_performed"` — the D-P14 gate performs no onchain observation), and the D-P10 private-read gate.
- **The chains demand different ceilings.** The receiver chain must be structurally ready AND current (fresh D-P6 and D-P8, D-P10 activated). The counterpart chain must verify WITHOUT issued identity: its D-P9 issuance maps `not_issued`, its D-P10 activation maps `not_activated`/`activation_record_invalid` (fixture nulls), its mapping maps `not_established`/`invalid`.
- **The no-second-identity widening proof.** A counterpart D-P9 record claiming the issued literals greens the frozen issuance assessor (probed before pinning: `fixture_structural_local_principal_id_issued`, `all_issuance_checks_satisfied`) — the frozen contract cannot refuse what it is designed to accept — and the D-P14 gate's own `not_issued` chain pin refuses it (`counterpart_chain_not_structurally_verified`) while the mapped echo stays honest and shows the issued claim verbatim. No second identity is ever created, accepted, or inherited (agent law L118).
- **The receiver's D-P6 leg now matters.** The frozen D-P10 chain is dp5/dp8/dp9 and predates D-P6 — a stale receiver D-P6 observation is invisible to the frozen D-P10 gate (probed: D-P10 still greens over the stale arm's legs). The D-P14 gate re-runs the D-P6 leg itself and refuses `receiver_chain_not_structurally_ready_or_current` with the honest D-P2 diagnosis carried. Freshness is inclusive (age == maximumAge is fresh; +1 is not).
- **Inferred activation is declaration-refused, not chain-refused.** Arm 8's activation claimed from the two single-principal activations completing arrives with BOTH chains verified and the session fresh — and the declarative check alone unsatisfies: completion evidence never answers whether to widen the read.
- **Cause order.** Record validity first (the input inventory and the activation record, with the raw evaluation pair diagnosed even there), then the join, then the receiver chain, then the counterpart chain, then session currency, then declarative completeness, then the ceiling check — each cause reads its own mapped echo, nothing downstream of a cause is claimed (the D-P13 ladder mold, no defensive literal).
- **Echoes stay readable through broken legs.** The join echo, both chain echoes, and the collaborative freshness diagnosis are computed on every arm — a broken counterpart leg never unbinds the receiver's records; an invalid arm still diagnoses. The refused arms carry `multiPrincipalReadScopePosture: "not_included"`; only the activated arm carries the positive scope literal.
- **The forbidden-key inventory widened once.** The gate's deep-walk inventory is the frozen D-P10 base plus five keys this cut exists to refuse: `principalIds`, `principalRefs`, `counterpartPrincipalId`, `counterpartPrincipalIds`, `membership`. Plural-batch smuggling is refused at the frozen D-P10 gate's own inventory too (probed).

## Recorded deviation from the implementation plan

The plan drafted one arm exercising "declarative + target checks both unsatisfied" in a single entry. Implementing it split it into two honest arms rather than one overloaded arm: the declarative refusal is arm 8 (`inferred_collaborative_activation_refused`, gate-level), and the out-of-table target refusal is the admission matrix's second entry (`smuggled_lane_label_target_refused`) — a target outside the 13-label table fails the ADMISSION validator (`admission_record_invalid`), not the gate's target check, so the gate arm cannot honestly exercise both.
