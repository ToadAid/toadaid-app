## Load-bearing mechanism

- **The surface is keyed on the session-scope binding, not per-record re-admission.** A record composed inside the session (`composed_at ≥ established_at`) shares the session's freshness window — it cannot be stale while the session is live, and re-admission-equality would churn with the D-P2 60-second clock and empty the view within a minute of composing. Check 5 re-derives the binding from every held record's composed event time against the re-run establishment event; out-of-scope records (earlier session instances) refuse the presentation outright, fail-closed.
- **Honest confinement on scope end.** Where the surface-level read gate re-run is not live — retraction, expiry, refusal, and nothing held — the surface refuses to present (`conversation_surface_not_presented`); where the gate is down and shape-valid records are held, the surface ends (`conversation_surface_scope_ended_records_inspection_only`) and every record entry carries its honest mark: refused-because-trust-epoch records keep the epoch distinction (`out_of_session_untrusted_epoch_inspection_only_never_consumer_admissible`), every other refusal lands `out_of_session_record_not_admissible`, and admitted-in-session records (when the session was live) present with `in_session_verified_boundary_session_scoped`. Retraction vs expiry stays readable in the mapped establishment echo. The trust marks are presentation-only — a confined record admits nowhere, and no surface state ever converts one back.
- **The composed text never enters any assessment.** The admission and surface assessors see records and marks; the composed text lives in shell state only (module scope, never persisted), and the surface pairs texts to entries by input order — the assessment is byte-identical whatever the text said.
- **Agent posture is a reflection, not a read.** Per distinct addressed agent ref among the held records, the frozen D-P13 declared-mode routing re-runs through the surface's own seam over the same frozen D-P12 leg records the desk carries: the trading-desk agent (agent-0, receiver-held) establishes verbatim; the two declared D-P0 slot refs echo the honest refusal (`no_declared_mode_structural_record`); anything undeclared was already refused at record admission. No memory lane, no record read, no lane read.
- **Cause ladders, no defensive literals.** The record ladder: record validity first (raw evaluation-pair diagnosis honest even there), then session currency, then the D-P15 read-gate re-run, then the session-scope binding, then the declarative failures (trust epoch, declared agent ref, composed-text bound, refusal postures) with their unsatisfied check names readable, then all-satisfied. Dedicated declarative causes for trust epoch / agent ref / text bound keep the mapped-refusal readable; a single posture-literal tamper folds into `conversation_record_invalid` rather than `proof_incomplete` (recorded deviation). The surface ladder: record validity, then the surface-level gate, then declarative incompleteness, then all-satisfied.
- **Refused-basis discipline (L49-55).** Each of the five record refused-inference bases and four surface refused bases fails on its own basis check alone with every leg green — `receiver_conversation_record_proof_incomplete` / `receiver_conversation_surface_proof_incomplete` with the exact unsatisfied check name, the D-P14/D-P15 mold.
