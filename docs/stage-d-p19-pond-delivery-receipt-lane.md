## Goal

The receiver **records one `delivered` receipt — the only performable state this cut — over a currently performed D-P18 in-process dispatch**, assessed by one frozen ceremony (`assessPondDeliveryReceiptDecision`) whose core mechanism is the re-run of the frozen D-P18 dispatch decision over the same 15 dispatch keys at the receipt's own evaluation instant: the receipt records only when the re-run verdict is `dispatch_performed_session_scoped_in_process_no_receipt`. The recorded state is `receipt_recorded_session_scoped_historical_evidence`, frozen at issuance. The receipt proves delivery alone — never recipient agreement, task acceptance, capability authorization, action, payment, result correctness, grants, consequences, membership, admission, or authority. One receipt per dispatch: a replayed attempt records the refused basis `replayed_from_prior_receipt_decision` and refuses at its own declarative check; refused receipts store nothing; the receipt survives retraction as historical evidence and is never deleted or staleness-denied; external transport, signatures, chains, payment receipts, result proofs, consequences, agent replies, and grants all stay refused (their own lanes).

## Load-bearing mechanism

- **The receipt must re-run the dispatch it certifies.** The decision re-runs the frozen D-P18 `assessPondDispatchDecision` over the supplied 15 dispatch keys — via a dedicated 15-key projection (`fullDispatchLegs`), never the 16-key input spread, so the receipt record itself is never handed to the D-P18 re-run — at the SAME evaluation pair. The receipt refuses at `dispatch_reassessment_not_performed` with the D-P18 re-run's own verdict mapped verbatim into the echo family whenever the dispatch is not currently performed.
- **Three-depth honest echoes.** The D-P19 mapped echoes carry the D-P18 re-run's OWN fields, which carry the D-P17 and D-P15 re-runs' OWN fields: `mappedDispatchState`/`mappedDispatchReason`/`mappedDispatchEventDiagnosis`, `mappedCandidateState`/`mappedCandidateReassessmentReason`, `mappedEstablishmentState`/`Reason`/`Diagnosis`, and `mappedReadGateState`/`Reason`/`Diagnosis`, typed via quoted-index reads through the frozen `PondDispatchDecisionAssessment`. A refusal three depths down (retraction → establishment confined at the session leg, gate refusal surfacing through the candidate's reassessment echo) stays readable at its own depth — this cut mirrors the whole reassessment instead of re-declaring the D-P18 cause vocabulary.
- **The receipt event's own freshness leads after the reassessment.** `receipt_event_not_session_current` fires on the event's own D-P2-style diagnosis (`delivery_receipt_event_time_only` freshness basis, metadata → evaluation time → maximum age → future time, fresh boundary inclusive) after the reassessment check. Recorded deviation (the D-P18 lesson one ladder up): the receipt event is the newest event in the chain, so under one shared declared maximum age receipt staleness implies every older event is staler; the isolation is by ladder order. The expiry arms prove the contrast in the other direction: the reassessment refuses first while the receipt event's own diagnosis stays honestly fresh (gate-expiry age 34 901, intent-expiry age 55 901).
- **Historical-evidence preservation (frozen at issuance).** The recorded receipt is never re-assessed for validity, re-stamped, or deleted. The contract is stateless; the shell module (`ui/pond-receipts.js`) never re-assesses a stored receipt — re-reads present the recorded facts verbatim — and consults the D-P18 re-run ONLY to set the honest presentation mark (`in_session` while the certified dispatch is still currently performed, `inspection_only` afterwards). Retraction confines the dispatch but the receipt survives: count unchanged, recorded facts verbatim, mark inspection-only, never deleted.
- **Receipts are frozen at issuance, and the receipt event is the newest event.** The receipt event must postdate the dispatched event it certifies (`recorded_at ≥ dispatched_at` — the scope check `receipt_event_not_of_the_current_dispatch_scope`, both fields read via quoted-index through the validated metadata) even when the event's own freshness is green.
- **The declarative basis.** `receiver_observed_in_process_delivery_completed_not_inferred` is the only admitting basis; four refusal bases (inferred from dispatch posture, asserted by model completion, claimed by third-party observer, replayed from a prior receipt decision) refuse at the declarative check with `receiver_receipt_proof_incomplete` — unsatisfied `["receipt_basis_receiver_observed_not_inferred"]`, satisfied 6, every leg echo green (L49-55).
- **One receipt per dispatch is receiver-recorded module state.** `ui/pond-receipts.js` refuses a repeat receipt of the same dispatch decision by recording the basis `replayed_from_prior_receipt_decision` — the ceremony then refuses at the declarative basis check. A refused attempt stores nothing. An out-of-range dispatch index refuses before any input exists (`dispatch_decision_index_out_of_range`, no assessment fabricated).
- **The receipt vocabulary is canonical, and only `delivered` is performable.** The five canonical states (messaging L203) are declared verbatim as literals; `POND_STAGE_DP19_PERFORMABLE_RECEIPT_STATES = ["delivered"]`; the other four refuse at the dedicated cause `receipt_state_not_performable_this_cut` — no destination-resolution, refusal, or failure machinery is invented in-process.
- **The proof binding realizes the derived-evidence minimum.** `PondDeliveryReceiptProofBinding` carries `expectedDispatchDecisionVersion` (the frozen D-P18 ceremony), `expectedDispatchState`, `actualSubjectCandidateVersion` (the D-P17 candidate), `comparisonPerformed: true`, and a two-literal recorded `comparisonResult`. The three identity fields are validated constants of the binding identity; the recorded result is verified against the performed comparison: a record claiming `dispatch_reassessment_refuses_the_recorded_dispatch_identity` over a performed-again reassessment refuses at the dedicated cause `receipt_proof_binding_disagrees_with_dispatch_reassessment`. The subject identity is the record-level `deliveredToAgentRef` echo and the subject instant is `dispatchedAtEventEpochMs` — both compared against the reassessed candidate's own `addressedAgentRef` and the validated `dispatchMetadata.dispatched_at_epoch_ms`.
- **The receipt has no separate destination.** No destination input key exists; `deliveredToAgentRef` is an identity echo of the certified candidate's own `addressedAgentRef`. A tampered destination refuses at the proof-binding disagreement cause, never widening any audience.
- **The postures live in the receipt record.** `PondDeliveryReceipt` carries the retention, presentation, and authority postures plus `authority: "none"` — 12 exact keys, deep-walked against the widened inventory.
- **The forbidden-key inventory widened once.** `POND_STAGE_DP19_FORBIDDEN_RECEIPT_KEYS` = the frozen D-P18 union plus four keys this lane exists to refuse: `receiptSignature`, `receiptChain`, `paymentReceipt`, `resultProof` — exact-key deep walk on record and assessment. `dispatchReceipt`, `dispatchQueueEntry`, `dispatchRetryRecord`, `externalTransportRecord`, `deliveryReceipt`, `agentReply`, `chatMessage`, and `agentTaskAcceptance` stay forbidden exact keys from the D-P18/D-P17/D-P16 inventories.

## Recorded deviations from the implementation plan

1. **The proof binding carries the subject identity through the record's own fields, not a duplicated `actualSubjectAgentRef`.** The plan sketched an `actualSubjectAgentRef` proof-binding field; the implementation binds identity through the record-level `deliveredToAgentRef` echo and `dispatchedAtEventEpochMs` instead — one subject-identity fact per record, compared against the reassessed candidate's own `addressedAgentRef` and the validated dispatch metadata.
2. **`comparisonResult` is a two-literal recorded union, not hard-coded agreement.** The plan's binding validation pinned the result to the agreement literal; that would have collapsed a record claiming disagreement into `receipt_record_invalid` instead of the dedicated `receipt_proof_binding_disagrees_with_dispatch_reassessment` cause, weakening the derived-evidence contradiction-refusal arm (L113-124). Implemented: the three identity fields stay literal-validated; the recorded result is verified against the performed comparison.
3. **The receipt's evaluation pair is the shared dispatch-legal pair.** The receipt re-run consumes the SAME `receiverEvaluatedAtEpochMs`/`receiverMaximumAgeMs` pair as the D-P18 re-run (one evaluation pair per re-run, house rule since D-P14); the receipt event's own freshness is diagnosed relative to that pair via its own metadata.
4. **The newest-event freshness arithmetic is by ladder order, not by arm** (the D-P18 recorded deviation one ladder up): the receipt event is the newest event, so receipt staleness implies every older event is staler; the expiry arms prove the honest-freshness contrast (34 901 / 55 901ms).
5. **The fixture emission is keyed on the evaluation-instant NAME.** An evaluation-instant override is unrepresentable in emission; variations are expressed record-side and the generator refuses overrides loudly — the D-P17/D-P18 lesson carried forward.
6. **The retracted arm's third-depth echo reads the establishment cause, and the gate refusal surfaces one depth down.** The plan's selftest sketch expected the retracted receipt to map the gate cause `live_session_read_gate_not_live_activated_refused_or_not_fresh` as the third-depth readable cause; the implemented chain correctly reads `mappedEstablishmentReason` → `receiver_retraction_on_record` with `mappedReadGateState` → `no_active_live_session` and the gate refusal surfacing through `mappedCandidateReassessmentReason` — the honest depth placement, mirrored rather than re-declared.
7. **The ceiling family is 16 keys.** The plan's sketch enumerated the six does-not-prove keys plus inherited ceilings; the implemented family adds the receipt-own grant/consequence/membership/scope keys (`receiptEstablishesGrant`, `receiptEstablishesConsequenceOrExecution`, `receiptEstablishesMembershipOrAdmission`, `receiptEstablishesScope`) so the receipt lane's own ceiling is pinned on the receipt, not only inherited.
8. **The shell-drive receipt instant sits inside the shared freshness horizon.** The D-P6 observed event is pinned at 1_800_000_030_000; the drive establishes at 60s, composes at 61s, prepares at 62s, dispatches with the dispatch event at 61.75s evaluated at 62s, records the receipt with the receipt event at 61.9s evaluated at 62s, and retracts/confines at 64s/65s — the D-P16/D-P18 drive's own horizon.
9. **The receipt-state refusal ladder places the state check after the reassessment.** The plan's ladder sketch was provisionally ordered with the state check earlier; the implemented ladder places `dispatch_reassessment_not_performed` second (after record-form) so an unusable-state receipt over a broken dispatch reports the broken dispatch, not the state — the more honest diagnostic order; the dedicated state cause is still reachable over performed dispatches (proven by the four state-refusal arms).
