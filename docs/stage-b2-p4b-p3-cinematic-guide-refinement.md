## Purpose

P4B-P3 refines the already-admitted procedural Three.js guide from the compact
P4B-P2 sentinel/robot-monk silhouette toward a calmer cinematic robed guardian.

This cut is still procedural and presentation-only. It does not admit a model,
texture, image, or other binary visual asset.

## Silhouette refinement

The P4B-P2 cylindrical body and rectangular front panel are retired.

The refined guide uses:

- a 32-segment LatheGeometry robe with a wide, layered hem and narrowed upper
  profile for a longer draped silhouette;
- rounded shoulder mantle plus a softer front shoulder drape;
- scaled cloth sleeves instead of exposed narrow cylinder arms;
- smaller, calmer hands placed closer to the robe front;
- a wider, softer frog head and muzzle with reduced eye and pupil scale;
- a subtler curved mouth;
- a thinner collar;
- an organic leaf-shaped chest sigil plus a bounded gold stem;
- slightly calmer, more elliptical halo composition.

The guide keeps exactly one bounded local point light.

## P4B-P3R1 native visual repair

The first P4B-P3 native proof established a healthy runtime and preserved the
world, but direct visual review found three bounded presentation defects:

- eye volumes were too deeply embedded in the head silhouette and did not read
  clearly from the front;
- the rotational robe volume still read too much like a rounded vessel rather
  than draped cloth;
- hands and the leaf sigil sat too low and appeared visually detached.

R1 moves the eye assemblies forward without enlarging them, adds one fixed
front cloth ShapeGeometry panel over the existing 3D robe volume, and raises
the hands plus leaf sigil/stem into the robe composition.

No world, authority, runtime, asset, loader, dependency, or capability boundary
changes in this repair.

## Motion

The motion remains deliberately subtle and inherits the existing global
reduced-motion time scale:

- smaller vertical presentation drift;
- very small whole-guide orientation drift;
- smaller head and eye-root motion;
- slight sleeve settling motion;
- slower counter-rotating halo rings.

No geometry or material is allocated per frame.

## World integration

The guide remains centered over the existing lily/platform presentation without
changing the world, shell, or authority projection.

P4B-P1 organic islands, water shader, ribbon waterfalls, bounded spray, stars,
moon, motes, fog, and holographic depth remain outside this refinement.
