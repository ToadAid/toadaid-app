# Stage D-P10 — private-read activation as a receiver-explicit session-scoped gate over structural records

## Binding

- Parent: toadaid-app main `fc64dcf` (D-P9 merged).
- Branch: `feat/stage-d-p10-private-read-activation`.
- Canonical ecosystem law: ToadAid/toadaid-architecture at `bc7a971dfb243f0aa4417da6cef85cc56204f783` — re-verified locally this cut. The evidence-activation contract makes activation **a separate governance transition** (`candidate → verified candidate → a separate governance boundary → activated with explicit effective capabilities`), requires at an authority-affecting boundary that verification be "current, applicable to the production subject, and independently inspected" (`activation_denied` otherwise), and states plainly that completion evidence does not answer "Should this capability be activated?" — so no identity-chain readiness activates anything by itself. The capability-authority boundary makes every read a capability (Invariant 1: `can_do(X)` does not imply `may_do(X)`) and requires any authority widening to be "attributable to trusted runtime policy or explicit governance"; the delegated-authority contract (L414: "A Grant is necessary only where the applicable policy requires delegated authority") leaves the principal's own reads a **non-grant space**, so the activation record attributes itself to receiver trusted-runtime policy and creates no Grant, no GrantId, and no grant lifecycle — the same Tommy-approved non-grant decision as the app-side cuts before it. Scope laws hold as postures throughout (law 1 "Memory never grants permission", law 6 "Personal state is private by default", law 7 "Release is explicit"); law 9 ("Temporary authority does not silently survive restart") is modeled as `session_scoped_receiver_restart_ends_activation`. The identity law "authentication is not authorization" pins the shape: the verified knowledge factor beneath the chain is identity evidence, and the activation decision sits on top, separately.

## Goal

Close Stage D's last refused lane **for the single-principal structural-records read class only**: the receiver explicitly records the activation of its own private reads — reading its own already-carried principal-structural records — inside a session-scoped, restart-expiring, revocable, non-grant ceremony. The activated read is honest about its ceiling in both directions: it is **not** inferred from the D-P9 `structurally_ready_private_reads_still_refused` ceiling (activation is the separate governance transition the evidence-activation law names, decided explicitly by the receiver), and it is **not** a content read — the read class is exactly `principal_structural_record_read` over the receiver-record table's six pinned labels, whose evidence source is the re-run of the receiver's own frozen upstream assessors at the composition, which IS the independent inspection the evidence-activation law (L109) requires.

## Proof model

- **Explicit decision, refused bases stay literal.** The activation's satisfy-able basis is `receiver_explicit_activation_not_inferred`; the ceremony carries the vocabulary of what it refuses — `inferred_from_structural_readiness` (the completion-evidence arm), `inferred_from_identity_chain_completion`, `derived_from_issuance_or_mapping`, `inferred_from_operator_prompt` — and every one is a valid record whose proof stays incomplete: never activated, never a governance decision from evidence alone.
- **Non-grant trusted-policy attribution.** The record self-describes via `trustedPolicyAttributionPosture: "activation_attributable_to_receiver_trusted_runtime_policy_no_grant"` and `grantSufficiencyPosture: "activation_requires_no_grant"`; the assessment carries the new `activationEstablishesGrant: false`. There is no GrantId key anywhere in the vocabulary, and `grantId` joined the forbidden-key inventory.
- **Session-scoped, restart-expiring, revocable.** The activation event is diagnosed with the D-P2 freshness machinery (`activationMetadata.activated_at_epoch_ms` over the receiver's evaluated/maximum-age pair — the D-P8 comparison pattern verbatim) and the scope posture must carry `session_scoped_receiver_restart_ends_activation`; a stale or future event is `activation_not_session_current`, never a renewal. The read stays revocable by receiver retraction on both layers (`activation_revocable_by_receiver_retraction`, `read_revocable_by_activation_retraction`).
- **Structural-records read class only.** Admission's read-class vocabulary carries the one admitted class `principal_structural_record_read` and the four it exists to refuse — `lane_content_read`, `authority_record_read`, `credential_record_read`, `cross_principal_record_read` — and its target vocabulary is a fail-closed literal table of six `receiver-record:` labels (the D-P5 ceremony, D-P8 mechanic, both D-P9 records, the D-P9 readiness composition, and the D-P10 activation record itself). A read basis claimed from the activation is itself refused (`inferred_from_activation`): the gate opening is not a read request.
- **Admission binds the read inside the activation without re-deriving it.** The admission re-validates the activation record structurally — kind, version, explicit basis, capability, scope and non-grant postures — and stops there; identity-chain currency and freshness are the composition's exclusive job (the D-P9 layered-partiality precedent). No admission check re-derives the chain.
- **Truth posture stays honest.** The derived-evidence law's current-truth requirement (canonical source → ref/revision → direct read) is not met by session-carried structural records, so every layer pins `truthPosture` / `readTruthPosture: "structural_presence_only_no_current_truth_claim"` — the records are inspected, their presence is confirmed, and no claim is made that anything outside the session is currently true.

## Honesty mechanism — activation is a ceremony with a ceiling, not an unlock

The honest positive state is each contract's own vocabulary: `fixture_structural_session_scoped_private_read_activation` (ceremony), `fixture_structural_structural_record_read_admitted` (admission), and `fixture_structural_private_reads_activated_structural_records_only` (composition). The refused tuples stay all-false on every arm — and, exactly as D-P9 did for `principalIdIssued`, **no D-P10 assessment carries a `privateReadsActivated` key**: the frozen D-P0…D-P9 family's `privateReadsActivated: false` literals remain the only carriers of that name, and the frozen-widening selftest block proves the D-P5/D-P6/D-P8/D-P9 assessors still report their exact pinned tuples while the D-P10 runners prove the absence by `hasOwnProperty` over every matrix arm. `runtimeActivationPosture: "not_included"` everywhere — this is a vocabulary gate over structural records; runtime/MCP/provider activation remains its own future lane. Stage D's heading stays `— BLOCKED`: collaborative and multi-principal reads keep an explicitly named deferred lane, exactly as Tommy decided this cut.

## Authority ceiling

The activation authorizes exactly one thing: the receiver's own structural-record reads, this session, this principal, no lanes. On every arm: `effectiveReadScopePosture: "read_only_single_principal_own_structural_records_no_write_no_send_no_sign"`, `collaborativeReadScopePosture: "not_included_single_principal_reads_only"`, `memoryLaneAuthorityPosture: "activation_authorizes_no_memory_lane_crossing_or_release"`, `authority: "none"`, `runtimeActivationPosture: "not_included"`, and every refused tuple (`activationEstablishesGrant`, `readEstablishesGrant`, `credentialAdmitted`, `authenticationPerformed`, `principalIdAcceptedAsAuthorization`, `personalMemoryContentAdmitted`, `currentTruthAdmitted`) is `false`. Law 9 is operational, not decorative: the stale arm proves a one-millisecond-expired activation ends the gate while the chain beneath it stays fresh.
