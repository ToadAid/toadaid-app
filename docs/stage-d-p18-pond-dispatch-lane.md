## Goal

The receiver **dispatches a prepared D-P17 delivery candidate in-process** — one dispatch per candidate, session-scoped, receipt-free, transport-free — assessed by one frozen ceremony (`assessPondDispatchDecision`) whose core mechanism is the re-run of the frozen D-P17 candidate decision over the supplied candidate at the dispatch evaluation instant: the dispatch is admissible only when the candidate is **currently prepared**. The performed state is `dispatch_performed_session_scoped_in_process_no_receipt`. Re-dispatch refuses as a replayed dispatch; refusal stores nothing; receipts, external transport, consequences, acceptance, agent replies, and grants all stay refused (their own lanes).

## Load-bearing mechanism

- **A candidate can only be dispatched if it is still currently prepared.** The decision re-runs the frozen D-P17 `assessPondDeliveryCandidateDecision` over the supplied candidate with the SAME legs and evaluation pair (design-B per-assessment re-timing — the D-P15 reassessment, D-P16 surface, and D-P17 decision precedents); a candidate that has gone stale, re-stamped, invalid, retracted, expired, or out-of-scope refuses at `delivery_candidate_not_currently_prepared` with the re-run's own reason mapped verbatim into `mappedCandidateReassessmentReason`.
- **Two-depth honest echoes.** The D-P18 mapped echoes carry the D-P17 re-run's OWN verdicts: `mappedCandidateState`/`mappedCandidateReassessmentReason` plus `mappedEstablishmentState/Reason/...Diagnosis` and `mappedReadGateState/Reason/...Diagnosis` typed through quoted-index reads of the frozen D-P17 assessment. A refusal two depths down (for example the D-P17 delivered-record admission refusing a re-stamped copy as `conversation_record_invalid` inside `delivered_record_not_currently_admitted`) stays readable at its own depth — the D-P18 cut mirrors the whole reassessment instead of re-declaring the D-P17 cause vocabulary.
- **The dispatch event's own freshness leads after the reassessment.** `dispatch_event_not_session_current` fires on the event's own D-P2-style diagnosis (metadata → evaluation time → maximum age → future time, fresh boundary inclusive) after the reassessment check and before the scope check. Recorded deviation (the D-P17 lesson one ladder up): because the dispatch event is the newest event in the chain, under one shared declared maximum age no arm can show dispatch-stale-while-legs-fresh — dispatch staleness implies every older event is staler; the isolation is by ladder order and the mapped leg echoes stay honestly honest alongside.
- **Session-scope binding.** The dispatch event must postdate the establishment, the delivered composition, and the recorded intent (`dispatched_at ≥ established_at AND ≥ composed_at AND ≥ recorded_intent_at` — the intent/composition metadata read via quoted-index through the validated candidate), refusing `dispatch_event_not_of_the_current_session_scope` even when the event's own freshness is green.
- **The declarative basis.** `receiver_performed_in_process_dispatch_not_inferred` is the only admitting basis; five refusal bases (inferred from preparation, asserted by model completion, replayed from a prior dispatch decision, inferred from channel visibility, scheduled or queued automatically) refuse at the declarative check with `receiver_dispatch_proof_incomplete` — unsatisfied `["dispatch_basis_receiver_performed_not_inferred"]`, satisfied 6, every leg echo green (L49-55).
- **One dispatch per candidate is receiver-recorded module state.** The contract is stateless; the shell module (`ui/pond-dispatch.js`) refuses a repeat dispatch of the same delivery decision by recording the basis `replayed_from_prior_dispatch_decision` — the ceremony then refuses at the declarative basis check. A refused attempt stores nothing, so it never blocks a later fresh preparation.
- **The destination flows only through the candidate.** No separate dispatch destination input key exists; the destination is the prepared candidate's own `addressedAgentRef` (the D-P17 binding is authoritative). An undeclared destination never prepares, so the D-P17 refusal is what keeps the dispatch audience honest.
- **The postures live in the dispatch metadata.** The 15-key dispatch input has no other home for the basis or postures, so `PondDispatchMetadata` carries them: the basis, the event fields (`dispatched_at_epoch_ms`, `freshness_basis` `"dispatch_event_time_only"`, `currentness_posture`), the seven postures (transport / presentation / consumption / consequence / acceptance / evidence / authority), and `authority: "none"` — 12 exact keys, deep-walked against the widened inventory.
- **The forbidden-key inventory widened once.** `POND_STAGE_DP18_FORBIDDEN_DISPATCH_KEYS` = the frozen D-P17 union plus four keys this lane exists to refuse: `dispatchReceipt`, `dispatchQueueEntry`, `dispatchRetryRecord`, `externalTransportRecord` — exact-key deep walk on metadata and assessment. `dispatchedMessage`, `sentMessage`, `deliveryReceipt`, `agentReply`, `chatMessage`, and `agentTaskAcceptance` stay forbidden exact keys from the D-P17/D-P16 inventories.

## Recorded deviations from the implementation plan

1. **The dispatch basis and the seven postures live in the dispatch metadata.** The plan's 15-key input left no home for `dispatch_basis` or the posture family; `PondDispatchMetadata` carries them (12 exact keys: basis, event fields, currentness/transport/presentation/consumption/consequence/acceptance/evidence/authority postures, `authority: "none"`).
2. **No separate dispatch destination field.** The plan already planned this; implemented as planned — the destination is the candidate's own `addressedAgentRef`, the input shape is pinned in the selftest, and the contract text never names a destination key.
3. **The gate cause is inherited through the reassessment mapping, not declared as its own D-P18 literal.** A broken/retracted/expired gate surfaces as `delivery_candidate_not_currently_prepared` with `mappedCandidateReassessmentReason` carrying the D-P17 re-run's own gate cause (`live_session_read_gate_not_live_activated_refused_or_not_fresh`) — a two-depth honest echo rather than a duplicated cause literal.
4. **The newest-event freshness arithmetic is by ladder order, not by arm.** The plan asked for the reassessment to be checked before the dispatch event's freshness (implemented per plan), and the isolation of dispatch-staleness from leg-staleness does not exist under one shared maximum age: the dispatch event is the newest event, so dispatch staleness implies every older event is staler. The implemented expiry arms prove the contrast in the other direction: the reassessment refuses first while the dispatch event's own diagnosis stays honestly fresh (gate-expiry age 35 001, intent-expiry age 56 001).
5. **The fixture emission is keyed on the evaluation-instant NAME.** An evaluation-instant override is unrepresentable in emission (an override would compute one assessment over a different pair than the emitted pin), so variations are expressed candidate/metadata-side; the future arm pins the dispatch event past the shared evaluation instant. The generator refuses such an override loudly.
6. **The re-stamp arm's mapped reason is the D-P17's own reason.** The plan's selftest sketch expected the re-stamp arm to map `conversation_record_invalid`; the implemented `mappedCandidateReassessmentReason` correctly carries the D-P17 re-run's OWN reason `delivered_record_not_currently_admitted` — the `conversation_record_invalid` literal sits one more echo deeper and is not carried; the selftest proves both depths by running the D-P17 assessor directly over the reassessment legs.
7. **The ceiling family is 14 keys, not the plan's stated 13.** The plan text said 13 false literals but enumerated 14 (the extra key is `dispatchEstablishesMembershipOrAdmission`); the contract implements the full 14-key list and the tuple invariant pins it.
8. **The shell-drive dispatch instant sits inside the shared freshness horizon.** The D-P6 observed event is pinned at 1_800_000_030_000, so the legs' window closes at 1_800_000_090_000; the drive establishes at 60s, composes at 61s, records the delivery intent at 61.5s, prepares at 62s, dispatches with the dispatch event at 61.75s evaluated at 62s, and retracts/confines at 64s/65s — the D-P16 drive's own horizon.
