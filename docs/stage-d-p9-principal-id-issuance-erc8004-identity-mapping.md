## Goal

Give the receiver its first honest local PrincipalId issuance surface and its first explicit ERC-8004 identity-mapping vocabulary — the dedicated identity-mapping cut the delegated-authority law reserved, implemented **app-side only** (the same decision as D-P8; no law-repo amendment): the receiver explicitly identifies itself by declaring its already-held principal ref issued in place — **one binding, one ref, no second identity** — and it records an explicit, revocable, independently replaceable binding from its principal to an opaque ERC-8004 identity label. Both records are identity bookkeeping; neither ever becomes a credential, an authenticator, a session, a grant, membership, or authority.

## Proof model

- **Issuance in place.** The issued PrincipalId is the existing receiver-held ref (`principal:fixture:stage-d-p0:local-principal`) declared issued by the record's own `issuedRefPosture: "receiver_held_ref_declared_issued_no_second_identity"` literal and matched against the receiver-held ref the classifier is handed. No second identity artifact exists, so the whole claim-collision class is structurally absent.
- **Cross-class distinctness as receiver-owned hard-false claims.** The issuance record carries seven hard-false claims (`isAgentId, isErc8004AgentId, isWalletAddress, isGrantId, isAttestationId, isProviderSessionId, isDisplayName`) validated at record level; any flip invalidates the record. The mapping record carries the D-P0 relationship literals verbatim plus `onchainIdentityIsLocalAgentId: false`.
- **Refused bases stay literal.** Both contracts carry the vocabulary of what they refuse — issuance: `inferred_from_presence`, `derived_from_wallet_address`, `derived_from_observed_onchain_identity`, `derived_from_erc8004_binding`; mapping: `inferred_from_wallet_ownership`, `inferred_from_onchain_observation`, `inferred_from_provider_session`, `equivalence_claim_not_explicit_binding` — and every one of them is a valid record whose proof stays incomplete: never issued, never established.
- **Establishment separated from verification.** The mapping's opaque label (`erc8004:…` prefix only — no registry semantics, no chain schema) may be bound explicitly while the onchain verification stays honestly unperformed: the classifier's verification check is fail-closed by construction this cut, the positive `verified_against_onchain_evidence` vocabulary exists in the type and is unreachable, and the honest completion posture is exactly `onchain_verification_not_performed`. Fabricated, structurally complete evidence is supplied data and satisfies nothing; even the receiver-observed verification literal (`receiver_observed_onchain_identity_evidence` — the seam for the dedicated onchain-verification cut) verifies nothing without independently reproduced evidence.
- **Identity refs are prefix-typed and collapse-refused.** `agent:` refs never embed a `principal:` or `erc8004:` ref; `erc8004:` labels never embed either, and the agent ref always differs from the principal ref — an identity collapse (`erc8004:principal:…`) is refused as an invalid record before any check runs.

## Honesty mechanism — issuance is not credential issuance

The honest positive state is each contract's own vocabulary: `fixture_structural_local_principal_id_issued` for the issuance, `fixture_structural_receiver_owned_mapping` plus `onchainVerificationState: "not_verified"` for the mapping, and `structurally_ready_private_reads_still_refused` for the composition. The refused tuples stay all-false on every arm. The D-P9 issuance assessment deliberately carries **no `principalIdIssued` field** — the frozen D-P0…D-P8 family's `principalIdIssued: false` literals remain the only carriers of that name, and the frozen-widening selftest block proves the D-P5/D-P6/D-P8 assessors still report their exact pinned tuples with `principalIdIssued: false` on their own fixtures. The composition composes the D-P5 ceremony, the D-P8 verified fresh knowledge factor, and both D-P9 records into four readiness checks; where all four hold, structural readiness is where the identity chain ends and private reads still begin refused.
